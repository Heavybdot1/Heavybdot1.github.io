1. Southern Baked mac and Cheese

Prep Time: 15 Minutes Cook Time: 38 Minutes Bake time: 25 Minutes Total Time:1 hour 18 Minutes

Equipment

  • Large pot
  • Colander
  • Whisk
  • 8-inch cast iron skillet or baking dish

Ingredients

  • 3 cups water
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • ¼ cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup Colby-jack cheese
  • 8 oz. macaroni noodles
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon paprika

Instructions

  1. In a large pot, bring to a boil the milk, water, butter, and macaroni.
  2. Create the flavorful roux by whisking together flour, milk, and the soul food seasonings.
  3. Stir in the cheese to create the creamy sauce, then add the cooked macaroni and heavy cream.
  4. Bake until golden and bubbly, serve immediately, and enjoy!
